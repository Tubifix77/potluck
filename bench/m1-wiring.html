Wiring the CP2102 adapter to board A

For M1: the PC talks to board A through the adapter. B only needs power, and you unplug it during the test. Board A stays plugged into the PC by its own USB cable, which powers it.

Use the pins printed 17, 18 and G. Not the pins printed TX / RX on the other header: those are the console, already used by the USB cable. And never connect the adapter's 3V3 or +5V pins to anything.
step 1: loopback, adapter only step 2: ground step 2: board 17 → adapter RXD (safe) step 3: adapter TXD → R (1–10 kΩ resistor) → board 18, the risky one
The adapter's pins are drawn in the order printed on yours, top to bottom: 3V3, TXD, RXD, GND, +5V. On the board, the positions follow Espressif's official table, and clone boards usually copy it. Still: find each pin by its printed label before you push a wire on.

The three steps, one at a time

Step 1: test the adapter on its own

  • Plug the adapter into the PC. Nothing connected to board A yet.
  • One jumper from the adapter's TXD to its own RXD (the two pins next to each other).
  • Tell me. I send bytes and check they come back, which proves the adapter and its driver.
  • Remove that jumper afterwards.

Step 2: two wires, board talking and adapter listening

  • Adapter GND → board G (J1, the bottom pin on the 17/18 side; any G works).
  • Board 17 → adapter RXD.
  • Safe whatever the adapter's voltage is: the board sends 3.3 V into an input that accepts up to 5 V.
  • Tell me. I check A's frames arrive.

Step 3: the last wire, adapter talking to the board

  • Adapter TXD → resistor (1–10 kΩ) → board 18.
  • This is the only wire that can hurt the board: if the adapter outputs 5 V instead of 3.3 V, a bare wire would push it into a pin rated for about 3.6 V. The resistor limits the current enough to protect the pin either way.
  • No resistor? Some adapters have a small 3V3 / 5V jumper or switch; set it to 3V3. With neither, it comes down to trusting the seller's "3.3 V" claim, and that is your call.
  • Tell me. Then the M1 test itself: A reads a value from B, you unplug B, and the read must answer UNAVAILABLE.

Board pinout: Espressif, ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 v1.1 user guide, Header Block tables J1 and J3, read 2026-10-04. Frame link pins from the firmware's CONFIG_POT_SERIAL_TX_GPIO=17 / _RX_GPIO=18. Adapter pin order: read off the owner's adapter, 2026-10-04. Its 3.3 V signal level: the seller's claim, unverified.
